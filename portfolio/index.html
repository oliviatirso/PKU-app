Research Areas

Three research threads: reliable AI for health, trustworthy distributed computing, and the coding theory and algebra that underpin the distributed-computing work.

AI & ML for health

Recommender systems and LLM agents for PKU dietary management, LLM-based analysis of dementia-related behavior, speech biomarkers of cognitive decline, and RAG systems for public-health questions. My focus is safety constraints and measurable reliability.

Privacy & distributed computing

Coded computation against stragglers, private and secure distributed matrix multiplication, private information retrieval, and differential-privacy leakage in adaptive systems, analyzed with information theory.

Coding theory & algebra

Decoding lattices with Gröbner bases, label codes of lattices, algebraic decoding of q-ary codes, and Nullstellensatz certificates and code selection for LDPC codes in wireless links.

AI for Phenylketonuria (PKU)

Project lead
Kean University · 2023 – present · Recommender systems, LLM agents, mobile apps

People with PKU have to keep phenylalanine (PHE) intake within narrow, age-dependent limits for life, and today that means doing a lot of calculation by hand. Since 2023 I have led a multi-year program that turns clinical nutrition guidelines into safe, personalized AI tools, from a published recommender system to PKU Wise, a working mobile-app prototype built by student developers under my mentorship.

JMIR (review)safety-constrained hybrid recommender

Research contribution

  • Collaborative and content-based filtering for PKU-safe food recommendation (IEEE HealthCom 2024).
  • A safety-constrained hybrid recommender that combines cuisine-aware filtering with clinical-rule enforcement (under review, JMIR Formative Research).
  • A 60-scenario benchmark (newborns to adults, 5 age groups) comparing reference values from a deterministic rule-based calculator with GPT-4o outputs for milk volume, formula powder, protein and calorie targets.

Product & engineering (student team)

  • PKU Wise: a cross-platform app with barcode scanning (Open Food Facts lookup), a PHE meal calculator and an infant-care calculator (energy, protein and PHE targets), intake logging, PDF reports, and an AI recipe generator.
  • A research-prototype chat assistant that combines deterministic PHE, protein and energy calculations, including milk-volume and formula-mixing guidance, with embedding search (RAG) over a food database.
  • Prototypes on other channels: a Telegram PKU Food Checker and an n8n-based assistant.

Dementia & cognitive health

Collaborative research
Interdisciplinary collaboration · 2025 – present · Speech biomarkers, LLMs

Early signs of cognitive decline often show up in how people speak and behave, well before a diagnosis. This work reviews how speech signals are measured across languages and uses LLMs to help caregivers understand and respond to behavior.

Research contribution

  • A structured review of speech biomarkers for cognitive decline in aging, MCI and dementia across languages (Living Adaptations, Antwerp, 2026).
  • Automated dementia behavior analysis and empathetic mapping with LLMs (Kean Research Day 2025).

Why it matters

  • Toward low-cost, language-aware screening tools and better support for caregivers.
