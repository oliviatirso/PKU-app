PKU Wise: AI-assisted dietary management for phenylketonuria

Lead Mentor / PI
[Institution, e.g. Kean University] · [2024 – present] · Mobile app + LLM evaluation study

People with PKU must keep their phenylalanine (PHE) intake within narrow, age-dependent limits for life, and that means a lot of daily calculation. I lead a student team building PKU Wise, a cross-platform app that scans food labels and barcodes, estimates PHE and protein, calculates infant formula and milk requirements, generates PKU-safe recipes, and answers questions through a clinically grounded AI assistant.

Research contribution

Designed a benchmark of clinical scenarios (infants 0–6 and 6–12 months, children, teens, adults) based on PKU nutritional-management guidelines, and compared a deterministic rule-based calculator against a retrieval-augmented LLM on PHE range, milk volume, formula and protein targets.

Product & engineering

Flutter app (iOS / Android / web) on a Supabase backend. Features include OCR and barcode scanning, a food library with vector-embedding search (RAG), an LLM chat assistant, AI recipe generation, intake logging, and PDF reports for clinicians.
